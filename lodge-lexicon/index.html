Degree II · The Middle Chamber

You climb the winding stair into the Fellowcraft's chamber. Three new roots come to you: MIR (water, reflection, truth), RAS (flame, heat) and VEL (memory). And three new actions: MO (still), NE (mend) and KA (carry).

Words can now take a third syllable, a modifier. -ON makes a word stronger. -ES sends it far. The modifier is optional and always comes last.

There are 108 possible words now. Each part of a word still shows in what it does.
